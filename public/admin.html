Shares

Price is what the customer pays. "Was" is optional — set it above the price to show a struck-through original on the shop. Status controls whether a share can be ordered at all.

Discount codes

Customers enter these at the cart step. Percentages apply to the animals only, never to the delivery fee.

Site banner

Shows as a strip across the top of every page. Leave empty for no banner. Basic HTML is allowed — e.g. 10% off whole lamb this week with code <code>EIDLAMB</code>

Shop settings

Tax is a decimal — 0.095 means 9.5%. Radius is straight-line distance from the shop; anything beyond it is phone-only.

Opening a species for orders

Beef and goat ship as Coming soon. Their cut sheets are already built — set every share of that species to In stock on the Products tab and they go live immediately, no code changes.
