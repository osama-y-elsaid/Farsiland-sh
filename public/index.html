Questions

Before you order

Is the slaughter actually halal?

Yes. Every animal is slaughtered by hand by a Muslim butcher, facing qiblah, with the tasmiyah said over each animal. No stunning, no mechanical blade, no exceptions. You're welcome to witness it — call ahead so we can time it with your visit.

What does the 24-hour window mean?

When your animals are packed we call you, and your boxes go out inside the window you picked — never more than 24 hours after packing. Nothing sits in our cooler waiting on a route.

Can I order more than one animal?

Yes, and each one can be cut differently. Add a lamb cut for kabob and a second cut for roasts as two separate lines, or set a quantity if you want several done the same way.

Why can't I check out from farther away?

Fresh meat and long drives don't mix, and we'd rather talk through timing and coolers before taking your money. Enter any address and we'll tell you which side of the line you're on.

How much meat do I actually get?

Take-home weight runs about 25–30% below the hanging weight listed. Ask for boneless cuts and that number drops further; keep the bones in and it stays high.

Can I bring my own animal?

Yes — that's custom processing, priced per head, and it's a conversation rather than an online order. Same for deer and elk during hunting season. Use the contact form above.
